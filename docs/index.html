The portable orderbook

Bitcoin market inventory, verified where you choose.

Ordex turns independently sourced PSBT evidence into a portable inventory that compatible markets can discover, review, and verify. Chain truth comes from a gateway you trust; distribution comes from signed Nostr events.

Markets coordinate around evidence, not custody.

Ordex keeps discovery and verification separate from the wallet action that settles a trade.

Inventory withdrawal is not a Bitcoin cancellation.

Removing an order from one Ordex gateway removes its local projection. It does not spend a UTXO, revoke a copied PSBT, or erase a signed event from other relays. Consumers should always re-verify before settlement.
